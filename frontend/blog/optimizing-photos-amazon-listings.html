Aligning with Amazon's Image Guidelines

Grasping and abiding by Amazon's image regulations is fundamental. They require high-resolution, clear images with a uniform white background. These guidelines align seller interests with buyer expectations, fostering a dependable shopping experience. For example, images should occupy 85% of the frame, maximizing visibility and detail.

Meeting these standards is not merely regulatory compliance but a critical step toward enhancing sales performance. Poor-quality images can result in reduced visibility and lower search rankings, impacting your profitability.

FAQs

How do I edit a product photo for Amazon? Use online tools like our image editor for streamlined background adjustments and image enhancements.

What is the best image size for listing on Amazon? Amazon suggests ensuring images are at least 1000 pixels on their longest side with a minimum resolution of 72 dpi.
