Why Blur Image Backgrounds?

Blurring backgrounds is a technique that focuses viewers’ attention on the central subject. This method not only enhances the visual appeal but also exudes professionalism with minimal effort. For online sellers, blurring can highlight a product away from any distracting background elements. Designers use this technique to maintain user focus and streamline visual experiences in apps and websites. Creators in the digital space leverage blur effects for artistic storytelling and improved audience engagement.
