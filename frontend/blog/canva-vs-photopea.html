What Canva Does Well

Canva's strength is speed. You pick a template, swap in your text and images, and export — often in under five minutes.

Key Canva strengths in 2026

  • Template library: 600,000+ templates across every format and use case.
  • Brand Kit (Pro): Lock in your brand colors, fonts, and logo across all designs.
  • Magic Resize: Instantly reformat a design from Instagram square to LinkedIn banner with one click.
  • AI tools (2026): Magic Write, Magic Eraser, Dream Lab — all in Pro.
  • Video editing: Full timeline editor for short social videos with auto-captions.
  • Background Remover (Pro): AI-powered, works well on portraits and product shots.
  • Collaboration: Share a link, your team edits live.

Where Canva falls short

  • ❌ Cannot open PSD, AI, Sketch, or XD files
  • ❌ No real layer system — complex compositing is nearly impossible
  • ❌ Pro features require $15/month
  • ❌ Works poorly for detailed photo retouching

What Photopea Does Well

Photopea replicates roughly 90% of Adobe Photoshop's functionality, runs entirely in a browser, and is free.

Key Photopea strengths in 2026

  • PSD support: Open, edit, and save native Photoshop files. Also AI, Sketch, XD.
  • Full layer system: Group layers, adjustment layers, layer masks, smart objects, blending modes.
  • Advanced selections: Magic Wand, Quick Selection, Pen Tool, Select Subject, Select and Mask.
  • Retouching tools: Healing Brush, Clone Stamp, Dodge & Burn, Liquify filter.
  • Color grading: Curves, Levels, Hue/Saturation, Color Balance.
  • Completely free core: Every professional feature available without paying.
  • No install required: Works in any modern browser.

Where Photopea falls short

  • ❌ No templates — blank canvas every time
  • ❌ No mobile app
  • ❌ No collaboration or brand tools
  • ❌ Steep learning curve if you don't know Photoshop

Our Verdict for 2026

Canva is the default tool for non-designers. Its 2026 AI features (Magic Write, Dream Lab, Magic Eraser) make it a powerful all-in-one for content creators and marketing teams.

Photopea remains the best free Photoshop replacement available. If you need professional photo editing without an Adobe subscription, Photopea is the answer.

Use Canva if:

  • You create social media content, presentations, or marketing materials regularly
  • You want templates, brand kits, and design systems
  • You work with a team and need real-time collaboration
  • You're a non-designer who needs professional-looking output fast

Use Photopea if:

  • You need professional photo editing (retouching, compositing, color grading)
  • You work with PSD, AI, Sketch, or XD files
  • You know Photoshop and want a free browser alternative
  • You need pixel-perfect control and advanced masking tools

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Canva better than Photopea?

For design and content creation, Canva is better — faster, has templates, no design skills needed. For photo editing (retouching, PSD files), Photopea wins. They're different tools; comparing them is like comparing Word to Photoshop.

Can Photopea replace Canva?

No. Photopea has no templates, no social media size presets, no brand kit, and no collaboration tools. It's a photo editor, not a design platform.

Is Photopea completely free?

Yes — all editing features are free. Photopea Premium ($9/month) removes ads but doesn't unlock any editing capabilities.

Which is better for beginners?

Canva by a wide margin. You can create your first social media post in 5 minutes. Photopea's interface mirrors Photoshop — there's a real learning curve.

Can I open PSD files in Canva?

No. Use Photopea — it opens PSD, AI, Sketch, and XD files natively and for free.

Does Canva work offline?

No, Canva requires an internet connection. Photopea can cache for limited offline use but cloud saves still need connectivity.
