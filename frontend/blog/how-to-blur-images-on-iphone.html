Easy Steps to Blur Images on iPhone

Blog

By: ChangeImageTo.com Team · 2025-12-19 22:35:00 UTC

iPhone offers several ways to blur images, from built-in Portrait Mode to editing tools. Here's how to blur images on iPhone.

Method 1: Portrait Mode (while taking photo)

  1. Open the Camera app on your iPhone.
  2. Swipe to Portrait mode (available on iPhone 7 Plus and later).
  3. Frame your subject and tap to focus.
  4. Take the photo - iPhone automatically blurs the background.
  5. After taking the photo, open it in Photos app.
  6. Tap Edit in the top right.
  7. Adjust the blur intensity using the f slider at the bottom (f/1.4 to f/16).
  8. Tap Done to save.

Method 2: Blur existing photos in Photos app

For photos already taken (iOS 16+):

  1. Open the Photos app and select your photo.
  2. Tap Edit in the top right.
  3. If the photo supports it, you'll see a Portrait option with blur controls.
  4. Adjust the blur intensity using the f slider.
  5. Tap Done to save.

Note: This works best with photos taken in Portrait Mode or with compatible iPhones.

Method 3: Using third-party apps

For more control, use apps like:

  • Snapseed (free): Offers Lens Blur and Tilt-Shift blur effects.
  • Afterlight: Provides various blur filters.
  • VSCO: Includes blur and bokeh effects.
