FAQs

Can I Blur Just Portions of a Background?

Absolutely! Advanced tools often let you apply selective blur, enhancing focus on your main subject by softly blurring the surrounding area only.

Will Blurring Reduce my Image’s Quality?

Blurring, when done judiciously, enhances image quality by emphasizing your primary subject over the background. Misapplication might lead to undesirable effects, so moderation is crucial.
