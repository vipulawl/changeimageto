Facebook Feed Post Sizes 2026

Facebook supports multiple aspect ratios for feed posts. The key principle: portrait images get more screen space, which means more time in front of the viewer before they scroll past.

Portrait Post (Recommended)

1080 × 1350 px — 4:5 ratio. Best-performing format for single image posts in 2026. Maximum vertical space in the feed. If you're standardising on one size, make it this one.

Square Post

1080 × 1080 px — 1:1 ratio. Clean, renders well on all devices. Works well for product shots and symmetric graphics.

Landscape Post / Link Card

1200 × 630 px — 1.91:1 ratio. Standard for link preview cards. When you share a URL, Facebook pulls the Open Graph image at this ratio.

Facebook Cover Photo Size 2026

Upload at 1640 × 624 px. Displayed at 820 × 312 px on desktop; crops to 820 × 462 px on mobile. Keep logos, text, and faces within the central 820 × 312 px safe area to avoid mobile clipping.

For group pages: upload at 1640 × 856 px.

Facebook Profile Picture Size 2026

Upload at 800 × 800 px. Displayed as a circle at 170 × 170 px on desktop, 128 × 128 px on mobile. Always use a square image — non-square uploads are auto-cropped to a circle.

Facebook Stories and Reels Size 2026

1080 × 1920 px — 9:16 ratio. Applies to both Stories and Reels. Keep critical content within the safe zone of 1080 × 1680 px — UI elements in the top and bottom bands can obscure content outside this zone.

  • Minimum resolution: 600 × 1067 px
  • Max file size: 8 MB (photo), 4 GB (video)
  • Supported formats: JPG, PNG, MP4, MOV
  • Max video length: 60 seconds for Stories, 90 seconds for Reels

Facebook Event Cover Photo Size 2026

1920 × 1005 px — roughly 1.91:1 ratio. Minimum accepted: 400 × 150 px, but anything below 1200 × 630 px looks soft on desktop.

Tips for Sharp Facebook Photos in 2026

  • Export JPEGs at 80–95% quality. Facebook's re-compression is gentler on pre-compressed JPEGs.
  • Use sRGB colour profile. Wide-gamut profiles cause washed-out colours on Facebook.
  • Resize locally before uploading. Letting Facebook scale your image down often adds more compression artifacts.
  • Centre your cover photo focal point. Mobile crops cover photos aggressively — design for the central safe zone.
  • Portrait posts outperform square. 1080 × 1350 px takes up roughly 33% more vertical feed space.
