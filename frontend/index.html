Image Quality Checker FAQ

What is an image quality checker?

An image quality checker analyzes a photo for issues like blur, noise, pixelation, low resolution, and bad exposure, then returns a quality score so you know if the image is ready to use.

How do I check image quality online for free?

Upload a photo in the dropzone at the top of this page. Our free image quality checker scores sharpness, noise, resolution, and exposure in seconds — no account required.

What quality issues does the checker detect?

It detects blur (soft focus or motion blur), noise/grain, pixelation, low resolution, overexposure, underexposure, and compression artifacts that make photos look unprofessional.

What does the image quality score mean?

80–100 is good and usually ready to publish. 60–79 is fair and may need minor fixes. Below 60 means significant issues — enhance, upscale, or recapture before using.

Can I fix problems after checking image quality?

Yes. After analysis we link you to free fix tools such as enhance, upscale, and compress based on the issues found.

Can I check multiple images at once?

Yes. Use the bulk image quality checker to analyze up to 50 photos in one batch — ideal for ecommerce catalogs and marketing assets.

Is the image quality checker free?

Yes. It is 100% free with no watermarks, no credits, and no signup. You can check and fix images for personal or commercial use.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and other common formats. Maximum file size is 10MB per image for fast analysis.

Are my photos stored after the quality check?

Images are sent for analysis and are not kept as a permanent public gallery. Use original files you are comfortable uploading for processing.

How is this different from just looking at the photo?

Visual review misses soft blur, mild noise, and compression damage — especially at scale. Automated scores give a consistent bar for teams shipping dozens of assets daily.
